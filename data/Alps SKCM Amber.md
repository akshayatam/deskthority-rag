**Alps SKCM Yellow**, or **Taxi Yellow Alps**, is a clicky switch in the complicated [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]].

## Description

[[Alps SKCL Yellow]]---linear yellow Alps with LED recess---was the longest-running Alps SKCL/SKCM switch. Alps also made yellow clicky switches for a brief period; these switches are referred to as \"Taxi Yellow\" switches owing to their deep yellow colour compared to the typical bright yellow linear switches. However, SKCL Yellow switches have also been made in this same deep yellow colour;[^1] it is not clear whether the change in colour signified a change in specification or simply a change in materials.

The [[switchplate]] has so far been found to be grey full-height.[^2]
