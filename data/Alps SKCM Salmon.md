**Alps SKCM Salmon**, **salmon Alps**, or **pink Alps**, is a tactile switch in the [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]]. Salmon Alps is somewhat stiffer than [[Alps SKCM Black]] that followed it, but significantly smoother.
