**Alps SKCL Grey**, or **grey Alps**, is a linear switch in the "complicated" [Alps SKCL/SKCM series](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL/SKCM_series "Alps SKCL/SKCM series"). The switch is heavier than [Alps SKCL Yellow](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL_Yellow "Alps SKCL Yellow"), and is the replacement switch for [Alps SKCL Cream](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL_Cream "Alps SKCL Cream"). This switch was commonly used in [Zenith](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Zenith "Zenith") keyboards for the space bar.

This switch was present in the 1994 Alps catalogue.
