**Alps SKCM Orange**, or **Orange Alps**, is a tactile switch in the [Alps SKCL/SKCM series](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL/SKCM_series "Alps SKCL/SKCM series"). SKCM Orange appears to have been introduced in 1987, a year before [SKCM Salmon](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCM_Salmon "Alps SKCM Salmon") and [SKCM Black](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCM_Black "Alps SKCM Black").[[1]](https://deskthority.net#cite_note-MouseFan-1)

Orange Alps are found both with and without the Alps logo on the top.

Orange Alps has been sighted with what appears to be dry lubricant applied to the slider.[[2]](https://deskthority.net#cite_note-Think-2)
