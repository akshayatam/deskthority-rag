**Alps SKCL Amber** is a linear switch in the "complicated" [Alps SKCL/SKCM series](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL/SKCM_series "Alps SKCL/SKCM series"). This switch was only used as a spacebar switch for the Tandem 6526 keyboard and other Korean-made boards from the former Goldstar Alps partnership. This switch is known as the Korean version of [Alps SKCL Cream](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL_Cream "Alps SKCL Cream"). The switch features long tabs similar to [Alps SKCL Green](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL_Green "Alps SKCL Green") and has only been observed with tall grey switchplates.

Unlike [Alps SKCL Striped Amber](https://deskthority.net/wiki/Alps_SKCL_Striped_Amber "Alps SKCL Striped Amber") this switch does not have any extra markings.
