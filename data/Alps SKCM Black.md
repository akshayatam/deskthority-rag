**Alps SKCM Black**, or **complicated black Alps**, is a tactile switch in the [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]]. Complicated black Alps is well-known for its use in the [[Dell AT101]] series of keyboards.

Opinions on complicated black Alps switches varies. The force curve is unclean, lending the switch a rough feel that leads many to pan the switch.[^1]
