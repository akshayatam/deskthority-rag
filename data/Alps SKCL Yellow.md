**Alps SKCL Yellow**, or **yellow Alps**, is a linear switch in the \"complicated\" [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]].

## Description

Yellow Alps have an LED recess; the LED occupies the position where the tactile or click leaf should go. Typically this recess is left empty.

Yellow Alps appears to be the longest-running product in the SKCL/SKCM series.

Various shades of yellow were used over time. The most common colour is bright yellow, although some also used the same dark yellow shade as \"Taxi yellow\" clicky [[Alps SKCM Yellow|SKCM Yellow]] switches.[^1]
