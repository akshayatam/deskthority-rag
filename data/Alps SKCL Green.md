**Alps SKCL Green**, or **green Alps**, is a linear switch in the [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]].

Green Alps switches typically have an LED recess; the LED occupies the position where the tactile or click leaf should go.

Green Alps are highly regarded for being an exceptionally smooth linear switch.
