**Alps SKCM Orange**, or **orange Alps**, is a tactile switch in the [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]].

Orange Alps is the second tactile SKCM switch, and it introduced the wide Alps [[tactile leaf]] design. The previous (and first) tactile switch, [[Alps SKCM Brown]], used a modified switchplate actuator leaf as the tactile leaf, while orange Alps switches use a design based on the click leaf from the [[Alps SKCM Blue|blue Alps]] switch.
