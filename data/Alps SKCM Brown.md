**Alps SKCM Brown**, or **tactile brown Alps**, is a tactile switch in the [[Alps SKCL/SKCM series]].

Tactile brown Alps is the original tactile switch in the SKCM series. Tactility was introduced by placing a second, modified switchplate actuator leaf onto a dummy block on the opposite side of the switch. This arrangement has been reported to have a superior feel to the later design, but this design was to be short-lived. Tactile brown Alps has also been found with standard tactile leaves.[^1]

[[Alps SKCL Brown|Linear brown]] switches also exist; these can (typically?) be identified by an LED cutout in the upper shell.
